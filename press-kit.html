ONE-SENTENCE DESCRIPTION

VitalCore Hospital is a hospital builder and doctor game where players run a living hospital from above and walk its corridors in first person to treat patients themselves.

SHORT DESCRIPTION

Build rooms and departments, hire and schedule staff, manage patient flow, beds and finances, then step into first person to interview, examine, diagnose and treat patients. The Steam Playtest is an active alpha.

CURRENT DEVELOPMENT SNAPSHOT

Alpha 0.9.199 is live on Steam Playtest. Recent shipped updates include more ear and throat conditions and scope views, staff handovers and sick leave, observation and medicine rounds, bed tracking, and fixes from tester reports.
