THE GAME TODAY

One hospital.
Many decisions.

VitalCore Hospital combines the builder, the administrator and the doctor in the same playable hospital. These are systems present in the alpha, with balancing and polish still in progress.

01 / BUILD

Make a place that works.

Draw rooms and floors, place equipment, connect storeys with lifts, build paths and parking, or begin with a staffed, ready hospital.

02 / RUN

Keep care moving.

Hire and schedule staff, manage beds and supplies, watch patient flow and finances, and respond when cases turn urgent.

03 / TREAT

Take a patient yourself.

Walk in first person, interview and examine patients with clinical tools, order tests, read results, diagnose and decide on treatment.

CURRENT STEAM PLAYTEST BUILD

Alpha 0.9.200
is live.

Build 26200323 was published October 25. This update targets large-hospital performance, urgent-patient alerts, staff schedules, bug-report housekeeping, and visual and vehicle fixes. The gallery above contains earlier development captures, not footage of this build.

0.9.200 / PERFORMANCE

Room for bigger hospitals

Optimizations to floor tints, reflections, scenery, door signs and build mode improve large-hospital frame rates. The developer's tower test rose from about 27 FPS to over 60 FPS at 2560 × 1440; results will vary by PC and save.

0.9.200 / OPERATIONS

Respond and schedule

Click the red alert ribbon to jump to a critical patient and open their chart. Staff schedules now span the full week, show actual hours, and include a “Fill the gaps” button.

0.9.200 / POLISH

Clearer care

Ambulances target the ER doors, stretchers carry a lengthwise patient on a gurney, and floor views hide the storeys above. First-person shading artifacts were removed, and F8 reports now clean up uploaded desktop zips.

DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP

What comes
next.

This is the current direction, not a promise of release dates. The December 2026 target in the project roadmap is a focused, polished demo slice, not the complete hospital simulation.

NOW / ALPHA

Tester fixes & first-person polish

Work through reports, improve the chart’s exam and interview pages, and make completed lab and imaging results easier to notice.

NEXT / VALIDATION

Verify the full care loop

Hand-play inpatient beds and doctor rounds, tighten the morgue kit, improve build feedback and check longer sessions across different hospitals.

PLANNED / DEMO

A focused first shift

A small clinic, patient admissions and visible staff work in a guided twenty to thirty minute scenario. The project roadmap targets December 2026 for this demo.

LATER / EXPANSION

More ways to practice

Additional specialties, maternity, disease outbreaks and deeper hospital systems are planned beyond the current alpha. Scope and timing may change.
