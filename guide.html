Before you start

VitalCore is in alpha. Things will break, and your reports are what fix them. A few things are set up differently from the finished game while we test.

  • The hospital is open 24 hours. In the finished game a new clinic opens 9 to 5 and longer hours come with each rank. For testing, every hospital is open day and night and all staff are on duty around the clock. Because of this, the Skip Night button and the end-of-day card do not appear.
  • Difficulty does nothing yet. You can pick Standard, Relaxed or Expert on the New Game page, but every game plays the same for now.
  • Random emergencies are off. Fires, power cuts and security events are switched off by default. Turn them on in Settings, under Interface, if you want them.
Found a bug? Press F8. The game saves a zip file to your Desktop with a screenshot, the log and your saves. Post it in the #bug-reports channel on Discord and say what you were doing.

Saving

  • There are three save slots. Press Esc and choose SAVE GAME. The slot button under it picks which slot you save into.
  • F5 quick-saves into the slot you are using. F9 quick-loads it.
  • The game also autosaves every 15 minutes of play into three separate autosave files. They never write over your own slots. Load the newest one from LOAD HOSPITAL on the main menu.
  • Updates never delete your saves. Older saves keep loading in new versions.

Your first hour

A new game puts you on an empty lot of grass with $150,000. Your hospital starts as an Urgent Care: a small walk-in clinic for mild cases. Your job is to build it, staff it and open the doors. These are the steps in order.

The game teaches this too. A tutorial card at the top of the screen walks you through every step below, one at a time, and moves on by itself when you finish each one. It is on by default. You can switch it off on the New Game page, hide it with its HIDE GUIDE button, and bring it back with Shift+F1.
The quickest way to build. The checklist on screen has a PLAN MY CLINIC button that draws a complete starter clinic plan for you. The ROOM KITS tab in the build menu has ready-made rooms with walls, a door and all the furniture already inside. Using both, you can have a clinic standing in a few minutes.
  1. Learn to move aroundW A S D move the camera. Hold the middle mouse button and drag to turn and tilt. Roll the mouse wheel to zoom.
  2. Open the build menuPress B. Everything you build comes from here.
  3. Build the urgent care roomsYou need ten rooms: Reception, Waiting Room, Triage, Exam Room, Doctor's Office, Administrator Office, Staff Break Room, Restroom, Supply Room and Janitor Closet, all joined by a hallway. Add a Blood Draw room and a Laboratory too, so you can do blood tests. Place each one from ROOM KITS, or build it yourself (see Building). Click a room to see what it still needs.
  4. Give the hallway a way inPatients walk in from the street. Make sure your hallway or reception has a door to the outside, and that every room has a door onto the hallway.
  5. Build three car parksOpen the OUTSIDE tab and place a Compact Patient Parking, a Compact Staff Parking and a Compact Ambulance Bay. Turn each one with R so its driveway faces a street. The doors will not open without all three.
  6. Hire your first staffPress H. A good first team is a Receptionist, two Nurses, a Doctor, a Physician Assistant, a Phlebotomist, a Lab Technician, a Janitor and a Porter. You can only hire nurses once the Triage room works, so build first and hire second.
  7. Check your suppliesYour Supply Room shelves start full. After that, the game never reorders for you. Press F3 and open Supplies to see what is left and buy more. A truck brings them about two game hours later.
  8. Open the doorsPress OPEN THE DOORS under the clock at the bottom right. If it will not press, it tells you what is still missing. Patients start arriving and keep coming.
  9. Watch your first patientPress P to see who is in the building. Follow them from reception to triage to the exam room and out the door. Your staff do all the work on their own. You only step in when you want to (see Treating patients yourself).
  10. Climb the LadderClick the rank badge at the top right. It lists what you must do to become a Small Clinic. When every box is ticked, press REQUEST INSPECTION. Each new rank unlocks new rooms, staff and patients.

First person

Press F to step into the hospital at the spot under your mouse. Press F again to go back to the overhead view.

  • W A S D walk, Shift runs, and the mouse looks around. Doors open for you.
  • Look at a patient and press E to raise their chart on your tablet.
  • Look at the desk in the Administrator Office and press E to sit at the computer.
  • Anywhere else, E raises your hospital tablet. The game holds still while the tablet is up.
  • B builds from first person. The game pauses, you pick a piece, then aim and click to place it.
  • Inside a lift, press a number key to choose a floor.

Reading the screen

  • Money and clock, bottom right. The money turns amber below $20,000. Click the money to open the finance window. The speed buttons sit beside the clock.
  • Open the doors, under the clock. You press it once, when your clinic is ready.
  • Department bar, along the bottom. Green means a department is working. Amber means something is missing. Grey means it is not built. A padlock means your rank is too low. Click a department to see its rooms and its day and night staff.
  • Medical Board News, the strip at the very bottom. Click it to read the news.
  • Floor strip and pins, bottom left. It shows which floor you are on. The PINS switch shows each room's name.
  • Clean percentage, bottom left. Click it to see which rooms need cleaning.
  • Red alert ribbon, top centre. It warns about critical patients, people stuck at the door, no free beds and missing staff. Click it to open the list, then click a line to jump to the problem.
  • Rank badge, top right. Click it to see the Ladder: your goals for the next rank.
  • Selected patient card. It has buttons for the chart, taking over care, letting the doctor handle it, calling a nurse, and more.
  • Room panel. Click a room to see its size, its doors, the furniture it has, and whether it is VALID. If not, it says what is missing.

Building a room by hand

  1. Lay the floor. In STRUCTURE, choose Build foundations and drag a rectangle.
  2. Wall it in. In WALLS, drag around the edge.
  3. Add a door. In OPENINGS, point at a wall and click. Doors have a sign strip, so you can pick the sign over the door or let it name itself after the room.
  4. Mark the room. Click ROOMS, choose the room type, and drag over the floor. This only labels the floor. It does not build walls. Dragging next to a room of the same type joins them.
  5. Furnish it. Click the room. The ITEMS tab narrows to that room and shows Required Equipment first, then Optional / Decor.

A room works only when all of these are true:

  • It is at least the minimum size for its type. The table in the next chapter lists them.
  • It is closed in by walls, windows and doors. A Reception and a Waiting Room may be open to each other. A hallway may be open where it meets the entrance or another hallway.
  • It has a door. Reception, Waiting Room and Hallway do not need one.
  • It holds everything on its required list.
  • It shares a side with a hallway, so people can walk in.

The room's panel tells you which of these is wrong, for example "Missing 1 × Sink" or "The room is not closed in."

Room kits

A kit is a complete room: floor, walls, door and all the furniture. Pick one from ROOM KITS, turn it with R and click. The price shows before you click. You can filter kits by department. To save your own design, use Save room as kit and click inside the room. Only kits you saved can be removed.

Furniture

  • Floor pieces stand on tiles and block them. Turn them with R.
  • Wall pieces such as clocks and cabinets hang on a wall. Point at the wall to place them.
  • Desk-top pieces such as computers sit on a desk or bench.
  • Loose pieces such as chairs and the surgical lamp do not block tiles. Put a chair in front of its desk yourself. Nothing snaps into place, and the desk finds its chair by where it stands.
  • Some pieces have colour choices. Pick one from the swatches before placing.

Moving, demolishing and undoing

  • MOVE picks up a piece of furniture. Click a clear spot to put it down, or right-click to put it back.
  • DEMOLISH removes what you click. Drag a box to remove walls, doors, windows, floor and furniture together. A floor with furniture on it must be cleared first.
  • Selling or demolishing gives back half of what the piece cost.
  • Ctrl+Z undoes building while the build menu is open.

Upstairs floors and lifts

  • PgUp and PgDn change the floor you see and build on.
  • Add upstairs floor builds one level above your highest floor.
  • Every upstairs tile needs a floor or a pillar on the level below, no more than two tiles away. Pillars stand on the ground floor only.
  • There are no stairs. People move between floors by lift. Mark a Lift room on the ground floor and the same tiles on the floor above, and give each floor a lift door. You can ride the lift yourself in first person.
  • The roof builds itself over any enclosed room.

Outside

  • Car parks. You need a Compact Patient Parking, a Compact Staff Parking and a Compact Ambulance Bay before the doors open. Each lays its own driveway. If it cannot reach a street, turn it with R.
  • Walkways come in five surfaces. Drag them across the lawn.
  • Grounds pieces such as trees, hedges, benches and street lamps go on the lawn.
  • The town builds the roads, pavements and bus stops. You cannot place those.

Departments

Every room belongs to a department. Most rooms pick their department by themselves: an X-ray room belongs to Radiology, for example. The bar along the bottom shows each department and whether it works.

  • Click a department to open its board. It shows the rooms it needs, which of them work, and its day and night staff.
  • Plan rooms from the board. Pick a room and drag on the ground to plan it, even before you lay a floor. The plan shows where to build.
  • ASSIGN ROOMS gives rooms to that department. Use it for shared rooms like a ward, an ICU, a nurse station or a waiting room. REMOVE ROOMS takes them away again.
  • Each department can have its own waiting room. A patient waiting for an X-ray sits in Radiology's waiting room. An untagged waiting room takes the department of the rooms around it.
  • You can hire into a department once its rooms are built and furnished. It only takes patients once it has staff too.

Press H to hire. You see four candidates for the job you pick. Each has a star level from 1 to 5, a wage and up to three traits. You can pay $2,500 to see a new set, or $1,000 to reveal their traits before you hire. New hires travel in from home by car, bus or on foot, so they take a little while to arrive.

Doctor roles

Doctors are one job with up to four roles each. You give a doctor a role on their staff card, in the ROLES section. Better doctors can take harder roles.

Doctor's starsRoles they can take
1 starEmergency, General practice
2 starsInternal medicine, Radiology, Infectious diseases
3 starsAnaesthesia, Cardiology, Neurology, Orthopaedics, Traumatology and the other specialties
4 starsSurgery, Intensive care

A doctor with the Radiology role reads scans. Without one, another doctor reads them more slowly. Surgery needs one doctor with the Surgery role and one with Anaesthesia.

Shifts, breaks and wages

  • Press F6 for the staff list. The day shift runs 07:00 to 19:00 and the night shift 19:00 to 07:00. You can also set someone to Off or On call. While we test, all staff work around the clock.
  • Staff get hungry, tired, stressed and need the toilet. They use the Staff Break Room and the Restroom. Without those rooms they wear out faster.
  • Traits change how people work. A Fast nurse works quicker, a Clumsy one makes mistakes, a Tidy janitor cleans better.
  • Good staff sometimes ask for a raise. You can accept, offer less, or say no. Saying no can make them work worse or quit.
  • If you cannot pay wages for three days in a row, staff start to resign.
  • You can send staff on training courses. They are away for 2 to 5 days and come back with more stars or a new role.

A patient's journey

Your staff handle every step on their own. This is what they do, so you know where to look when something stalls.

  1. ArrivePatients come on foot, by car, by bus or by ambulance. Ambulances only come once you have an Emergency department. At most 40 patients can be inside at once.
  2. Check inThey queue at reception and a receptionist registers them. Very mild cases, such as a small cut or the flu, skip the desk and go straight to a waiting room.
  3. TriageA nurse takes them into the Triage room, weighs them, and takes their first vital signs: pulse, blood pressure, breathing, temperature, oxygen and pain. Then the nurse decides how urgent they are.
  4. WaitThey wait for a free exam room or bed. Long waits make them stressed and lower your reviews.
  5. See the doctorThe doctor talks to them, examines them, and listens to their chest. Each of these finds different clues.
  6. TestsThe doctor orders tests. Some happen at the bedside. Others mean a trip, by wheelchair or stretcher, to the room with the right machine.
  7. Diagnosis and treatmentWhen the results are in, the doctor names the illness and treats it. Doctors order medicines and nurses give them. Doctors do procedures such as casts and stitches themselves.
  8. Stay or go homeMost patients go home. Serious illnesses, like pneumonia, sepsis or a heart attack, need a stay in a ward bed. A nurse wheels them there and checks on them in rounds.
  9. DischargeA nurse takes a final set of vitals and does the paperwork. The doctor may print a prescription. The patient changes, pays, and goes home the way they came.

Surgery

From Regional Medical Center you can operate. You need a working Operating Room, a doctor with the Surgery role and a doctor with the Anaesthesia role. The surgeon, an assistant, the anaesthetist and a scrub nurse work together, and the patient then recovers on a ward. Without all three, a patient who needs an operation is sent to another hospital by ambulance.

When things go badly

  • Critical arrivals you cannot treat. If a level 1 or 2 patient turns up before you have an Emergency department, an alarm sounds. Keep them stable until the paramedics arrive in 60 seconds. A nurse does this for you, or you can step in yourself in first person: press E to grab the crash cart, then hold E to help them breathe.
  • Code Blue. A patient who collapses needs chest compressions within 45 seconds. The nearest doctor or nurse runs to them.
  • Deaths. Untreated serious illness gets worse over hours. With a Morgue, a nurse takes the body there.

Patients come back

Patients live in real homes in the town. About a quarter of new arrivals are people you have treated before. They grow older between visits, about a year for every three days of game time, and keep their history and allergies. Some arrivals are relatives of earlier patients, with the same surname and home. Their chart shows their family history. Press O to look anyone up.

Treating patients yourself

Every patient starts out doctor managed: your staff decide everything. You can take over any patient at any time and make the calls yourself. Your staff still carry out what you order.

From the overhead view

  1. Click a patient, then press E, or press TAKE OVER on their card. This opens the Care Desk.
  2. ORDERS and ASK: order tests and ask questions. Tests you cannot do yet are greyed out until you build their room.
  3. DIAGNOSE: see the clues found so far and a "?" for each one still hidden, with the test that would find it. Add the illnesses you suspect, then press SET FINAL DIAGNOSIS. CONSULT STAFF asks your most experienced doctor for their opinion.
  4. TREAT: choose the treatment.
  5. FINISH: watch and reassess, write a prescription, keep them in, send them to intensive care, or discharge them.

Get the diagnosis wrong and the patient gets worse for a while. The chart flags it so you can try again. Press LET DOCTOR HANDLE at any time to hand the patient back.

In first person

Walk up to a patient and press E. Your tablet shows their chart, with tabs for Overview, History, Vitals, Results, Medicines, Diagnosis and Care. Only the Overview opens before the triage nurse has seen them. On the CARE tab, press TAKE OVER CARE to get the full Care Desk. The hands-on exams there need you to stand beside the patient.

Stuck on a word? Press L for the Medical Library. It explains every illness, test, medicine and treatment in plain words. You can also click an illness in the diagnosis list to read about it.

Money and supplies

Press F3, or click your money, to open the finance window. It has four pages.

Money

  • Money comes in from patient bills, insurance payments, grants and food sales.
  • Money goes out on wages, running costs, room upkeep and anything you build or buy.
  • Short of cash? The Borrow button lends you up to $200,000 at 1% a day.

Prices

One slider for each kind of charge: consults, tests, treatment, surgery and food. You can set each from half price to double. Higher prices bring in more per patient, but fewer people come and they review you worse.

Insurance

Six insurance companies send you patients. Each pays a different share of the bill, takes a different time to pay, and turns down a different share of claims. You can accept or turn away each company. Turn one away and most of its customers go elsewhere. Insurers also set a daily target of patients treated, with a bonus when you meet it.

Supplies

The game does not order supplies for you. Staff use up stock with every test and treatment. When it runs out, work stops. Keep an eye on this page.
  • There are five kinds of supplies: Lab, Imaging, Surgical, General and Food. Each box gives 10 uses.
  • A truck delivers about two game hours after you order, and leaves the boxes at the door. A Porter carries them to the shelves. Without a porter, the driver does it more slowly.
  • Shelves in your Supply Room hold the stock. They start full in a new hospital, and more shelves hold more boxes.
  • Medicines are separate. With a Pharmacy and a Pharmacy Technician, the pharmacy hands them out and restocks itself from your cash.

Cleaning

Rooms get dirty from being used, not from time passing. The busiest rooms get dirty fastest. Click the Clean percentage at the bottom left to see the dirtiest rooms first.

Janitors take their cart from the Janitor Closet and clean a few rooms each round, on their own. Hire more janitors as you grow.

The Ladder

Your hospital climbs eight ranks. Click the rank badge at the top right to see the goals for the next one. When every goal is ticked, press REQUEST INSPECTION. The inspector checks and either promotes you or tells you what is still to do. Your star rating only counts once at least 10 patients have reviewed you.

Accreditation

Accreditation is how much inspectors trust you. It starts at 70 and changes at an audit every 30 days. Good infection control raises it, and preventable deaths lower it. Keep rooms clean and patients alive.

Reviews

Patients rate their whole visit: the wait, a seat, a clean room, the price, and whether they got better. Staff say what it is like to work for you. Press Shift+F to read both. Staff reviews do not change your stars, but they warn you before people quit.

The town and the story

  • Time. A game day lasts about an hour at normal speed. The calendar starts on today's real date.
  • Seasons. Trees are green in spring and summer, turn in autumn and carry snow in winter. Halloween and Christmas decorations go up on their own.
  • Weather. Some days bring rain, fog or a storm. A storm can cut the power. You can turn weather off in Settings.
  • The town. Cars drive the streets, buses run four lines from 05:30 to 23:00, and people walk between their homes and the shops. Your patients and staff live here.
  • The story. Old Town General's roof fell in, and yours is the last hospital in town. Medical Board News reports on you every morning, and letters arrive as you grow. Click the news strip to read them. The Letters window can read them to you.
  • The admin computer. Sit at the Administrator Office desk in first person to use the hospital computer. It holds every window in one place: patients, records, staff, schedule, hiring, departments, money, prices, supplies, the Ladder, news, the medical library, letters and cleaning.

When something goes wrong

The Open the Doors button will not press.
The button says what is still missing. The usual causes are a room that is not finished, fewer than 2 waiting seats, no janitor, a missing core job, or a missing car park. You need all three: patient parking, staff parking and the ambulance bay.
I cannot hire a nurse.
Nurses need a working Triage room first. Click your Triage room and add whatever it says is missing. The same rule applies to every job: "You need somewhere for them to work first" means a room in that department is missing or unfinished.
My room says it is not working.
Click the room. Its panel says exactly what is wrong. The usual causes are a gap in the walls, no door, a piece of required furniture missing, or the room being too small. A room must also touch a hallway.
My room says "Missing", but the item is right there.
Check it is the right item. The room's list in the ITEMS tab shows the exact pieces that count. Desk-top items like computers must sit on the desk.
Patients keep getting sent away.
They are sicker than your rank allows. Climb the Ladder, and build an Emergency department at Community Hospital to keep level 1 and 2 patients.
Work has stopped and nobody is doing tests.
You have probably run out of supplies. Press F3, open Supplies, and buy more. Hire a Porter so deliveries reach the shelves quickly.
Nobody does surgery.
You need a working Operating Room, a doctor with the Surgery role and a doctor with the Anaesthesia role. Give doctors roles on their staff card. Surgery needs a 4-star doctor.
The game will not let me build upstairs.
Build the floor below first. Every upstairs tile also needs a floor or a pillar underneath, no more than two tiles away. People reach upstairs rooms by lift, so add one.
A car park will not go down.
Its driveway has to reach a street. Turn it with R, or move it closer to a road and away from traffic lights.
My staff are slow and grumpy.
Check they have a Staff Break Room and a Restroom. Hire enough people that nobody works alone, and look at their reviews with Shift+F.
Something else is broken.
Press F8 and post the zip from your Desktop in #bug-reports on Discord, with a sentence about what happened. That is the fastest way to get it fixed.
